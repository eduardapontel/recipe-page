Coffee Cupcakes with Whipped Cream

The perfect little treat to enjoy with your everyday coffee. They're easy to make and turn out wonderfully every time. Give them a try and let me know what you think!

Time: 1 hr 10 min
Yield: 12 cupcakes
Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 1 tablespoon instant coffee
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 cups prepared whipped cream
  • Coffee beans, for decoration
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon instant coffee
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons butter

Preparation

In a blender, combine the warm water, instant coffee, eggs, oil, sugar, and cocoa powder. Blend until smooth. Transfer the mixture to a bowl, add the flour and baking powder, and mix with a spoon until well combined.

Pour the batter into cupcake liners and arrange them side by side in a large baking pan. Bake in a preheated oven at medium heat for about 25 minutes, or until the cupcakes are baked through and golden brown. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely.

For the filling, combine all the ingredients in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens. Remove from the heat, let it cool, then transfer it to a piping bag fitted with a round tip. Set aside.

Transfer the whipped cream to a piping bag fitted with a star tip and set aside.

To assemble, make a small hole in the center of each cupcake and fill it with the reserved coffee brigadeiro. Pipe swirls of whipped cream on top of each cupcake, then decorate with coffee beans. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
